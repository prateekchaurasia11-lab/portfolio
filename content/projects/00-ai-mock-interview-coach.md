### The challenge

Interview practice needs feedback that is immediate, structured, and useful enough to help candidates improve across multiple sessions.

### The approach

- Built a full-stack AI interview platform with frontend flows, API routes, speech-to-text, database persistence, and LLM evaluation.
- Integrated Groq Whisper transcription and GPT-4o-mini feedback through OpenRouter to generate structured interview scores.
- Developed Supabase session persistence to track communication, confidence, and content-quality scores over time.
